Research Lab: Single-Frame vs Temporal Verification JUDGES BENCHMARK

Standard computer-vision assistive apps treat each camera frame as an isolated snapshot, causing relentless false alarms, bounding-box jitter, and severe wearer audio fatigue. SightWeaver's Multi-Frame Temporal Movement Verification tracks displacement vectors over an 8-frame temporal window and suppresses redundant alerts via Predictive Hazard Memory.

Welcome to SightWeaver

AI Smart Glasses Platform for the Visually Impaired

SightWeaver operates as an autonomous hands-free companion. You can say your destination once (e.g. "Take me to my classroom"), and the system continuously guides your footsteps with turn instructions and obstacle alerts.

Sensors & Permissions:
  • Camera: Analyzes obstacles and signs in real-time. No video is recorded to servers.
  • Microphone: Used only for voice commands when activated.
  • Location: Matches saved places and routes.

⚠️ Important: SightWeaver is an assistive aid and does not replace a white cane or mobility training.
